How it works

See it work — then, if you like, read exactly how.

Each step has a short clip. Nothing plays on its own; tap one to watch.

Your Daily Briefing

GoSosh opens on a briefing, not a feed. Five widgets — Trending Up, Cross-Source, Quiet Movers, Talk of the Town, and Most Mentioned — each answer a different question about your topics: what’s hot, what’s confirmed across multiple sources, what’s rising before it breaks, what real people are actually saying, and who’s being talked about most. Scroll one, swipe to the next. Read it, and get on with your day.

Track what you care about

Tap the sidebar and add a card for whatever matters: your name, a person, a brand, a topic. GoSosh fans out across Google News, RSS, Reddit, Hacker News, Bluesky, Mastodon, X, and YouTube, dedupes what it finds by story, and ranks it — so a headline carried by five sources reads as a stronger signal than one that isn’t. No account, no setup pain. It just starts.

Make it your own Plus

Plus turns GoSosh into your dashboard. Author Custom Topics that sit as chips next to News, Financial, Sports, and Tech; build multiple dashboards for different corners of your life; track tickers on the Stock Dashboard; and set Buzz Alerts that ping you when activity spikes past your threshold. A one-time purchase — never a subscription — with a 7-day free trial.

Read the original, not a summary

GoSosh doesn’t generate text, paraphrase articles, or make things up. Every tap takes you to what the writer actually wrote, in a clean in-app reader — no chatbot in the middle. The whole point is more time reading the source, less time talking to a robot about it.

What’s gaining momentum

The cross-source intelligence runs on your iPhone using Apple Intelligence — recognizing when several outlets are covering the same story even when they share no keywords, and surfacing who’s being talked about across sources. Nothing about what you read leaves your device.
